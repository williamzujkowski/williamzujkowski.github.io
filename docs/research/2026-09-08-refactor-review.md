Final verification uses the normal CI gates and the versioned manual full-site suite. Live assets should be compared with the exact uploaded deployment artifact: separate Pagefind builds may generate different index hashes. Issue #540 stays open until the upstream behavior is fixed; repairing one bot branch does not establish that future updates are safe.
