# Blog voice review

Read the voice section of [repository policy](../../../AGENTS.md) and the
[review contract](../../../docs/skills.md). Review the requested post as a whole;
resolve repository paths from the root, three directories above this skill.

## Procedure

Read for the precise, direct engineer voice with dry observational wit. For a
project post, check that the opening explains what the project is and why a reader
would want it before introducing detailed constraints. Preserve honest first-person
experience, purposeful sentence variation, and humor that improves the observation.

Locate filler, corporate hedging, academic transitions, repetitive paragraph
templates, manufactured drama, stock contrast formulas, overused three-item
parallelism, and multiple closing summaries. Cite the exact sentence and explain
what becomes clearer if changed. Review em dashes by function: a useful aside is
different from a dramatic reveal. Distinguish structural bold labels from scattered
emphasis. Do not replace precise technical nouns merely to increase vocabulary.

Assess passive constructions when they conceal the actor or make an instruction
unclear. A passive sentence with a useful focus can stay. Exclude code, quoted
source language, URLs, and bibliography entries from author-voice findings; check
the surrounding prose instead.

Sentence variation, vocabulary diversity, transition frequency, and punctuation
counts can help locate repetitive passages. They are optional editorial
heuristics, not reliable evidence of AI authorship, dishonesty, or quality. Do not
label writing machine-generated from these metrics or invent numerical results.
Apply the actual policy rather than expanding a heuristic word list into bans on
legitimate technical terms.

## Result

Give a ranked, line-numbered list with the passage, concrete readability problem,
and a small suggested edit when useful. Distinguish material voice problems from
optional polish; do not make a single harmless word or density threshold a
publication blocker. Report reviewed scope, coverage, and verdict under the shared
contract. Audit by default; apply edits when the task authorizes them, preserving
technical meaning and the author's experience. Attribution and factual support
belong to their dedicated stages.
